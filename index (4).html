About me

My name is Haris Ali. I am a video editor, graphic designer and YouTube automation expert based in Sargodha, Pakistan. I have three to four years of hands-on experience in YouTube automation, and my own channel, Five Sister Challenges, has a Short that reached more than 10 million views in 10 days.

For editing, I work in Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut and VN, and I use AI tools in my production workflow. I create voiceovers with ElevenLabs and design graphics in Canva. I have also spent several months working with colleagues on video editing and graphic design projects.

Alongside my professional work, I am studying for a BS in Computer Science and am currently in my fifth semester.
