About me

I am Haris Ali, a video editor, graphic designer and YouTube automation expert based in Sargodha, Pakistan. I produce video content and graphics for creators and brands publishing on YouTube and social media.

For the past 3 to 4 years I have worked in AI video creation and YouTube automation. A single video I produced for a client crossed 10 million views in under 10 days.

I also have 3 to 4 years of professional video editing experience. CapCut is my primary tool for both client and personal projects, supported by Premiere Pro and VN. I produce voiceovers with ElevenLabs and design graphics in Canva. I have also worked alongside colleagues on video editing and graphic design projects.

I am currently pursuing a BS in Computer Science (BSCS) and am in my fifth semester.
