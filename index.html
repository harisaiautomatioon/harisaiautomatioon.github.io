About me

I am Haris Ali, a video editor, graphic designer and YouTube automation expert based in Sargodha, Pakistan.

For 3 to 4 years I have worked on AI video creation and YouTube automation. For one client, I created a single video that reached more than 10 million views in under 10 days.

I also have 3 to 4 years of video editing experience. CapCut is the tool I use most, for client work and for my own projects, alongside Premiere Pro and VN. I create voiceovers with ElevenLabs, design graphics in Canva, and have worked with colleagues on video editing and graphic design projects.

I am currently studying BSCS and am in my fifth semester.
